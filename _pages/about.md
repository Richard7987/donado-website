I am a Professor-Researcher (Titular C) in the Academic Area of Mathematics and
Physics at the [Universidad Autónoma del Estado de Hidalgo](https://www.uaeh.edu.mx/)
(UAEH), Mexico. I earned my BSc, MSc, and PhD in Physics from the
[Benemérita Universidad Autónoma de Puebla](https://www.buap.mx/) (BUAP), and I
founded UAEH's undergraduate and master's programs in Physics and Advanced
Technology.

My research uses **fluidized granular matter** as a tabletop, macroscopic model
for phenomena that are otherwise only accessible at the colloidal and molecular
scale — crystallization, the glass transition, melting and superheating,
self-assembly, and active matter. A second line studies **magnetic fluids driven
by time-dependent magnetic fields**, where an external field sets the interactions
and lets us tune structure and flow on demand. The two lines meet in the same
question: how collective order and transport emerge from many simple particles
under an external drive.

This work is carried out with students and with colleagues in Mexico and abroad,
and has appeared in more than 50 peer-reviewed articles. I am a member of the
Sistema Nacional de Investigadores (Level II) and of the Mexican Academy of
Sciences (since 2018), and I serve on the Editorial Board of *Scientific Reports*
(Springer Nature) since 2022.
